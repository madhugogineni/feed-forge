# Content execution backlog

This file preserves ideas that MG may want to implement, test, build, record, or
turn into content. It is not a publishing queue and does not authorize posting.

Last updated: 23 September 2026.

## Ready to develop

### 1. AI capability scouting series

Find a surprising, reproducible capability in a current AI tool and turn it into
a small demonstration.

- Deliverable: a working example, short screen recording, concise post, and a
  line explaining why the capability matters.
- Good candidates: coding-agent skills, computer use, data visualization,
  content transformation, repository analysis, or useful automations.
- Gate: the result must be tested. Do not repost a vendor claim as a demo.
- User input: ask MG whether he wants to build the strongest candidate when it
  requires his account, device, or judgment.

### 2. Publishable skill or small tool

Propose and help build a reusable skill, script, or miniature tool that is useful
enough to publish and simple enough to demonstrate clearly.

- Initial directions: a Feed Forge evidence checker, an X post/media package
  generator, a credit-card offer catch extractor, an AI benchmark comparison
  helper, or a mobile-readable chart generator.
- Deliverable: scoped implementation brief, repository artifact, usage example,
  screen recording plan, launch post, and follow-up post based on feedback.
- Selection rule: prefer something MG would genuinely use even if the post did
  not exist.

### 3. Model-release reaction package

For every meaningful AI model release, prepare a package that separates the
vendor announcement from independent evidence.

- Capture: claimed capabilities, pricing, limits, availability, benchmark
  source, methodology caveat, and one comparable model.
- Draft shape: the result, the useful comparison, MG's opinion, and a conclusion.
- Media: one sourced comparison chart when the numbers are truly comparable;
  otherwise use text or a real demo.
- Source checklist: use `SOURCE_LIBRARY.md`.

### 4. RSS-to-one-line post lane

Convert a verified item from RSS or a first-party page into a concise post.

- The line must state what changed and why MG cares.
- Add a catch, implication, or opinion when the item comes from a company.
- Use an image or screenshot when the text would otherwise be too vague.
- Good topics: gadget prices, launch availability, bank-card changes, payment
  features, model releases, telecom changes, and market-share updates.

### 5. Consumer-tech market-share monitor

Monitor Canalys and other primary or specialist market-data releases for India
smartphone, PC, wearable, and related market-share changes.

- Deliverable: a short ranked post or one clear chart with period, geography,
  units, source, and any methodology limitation.
- Do not mix shipment share, installed base, and sales share.

### 6. Question-bank lane

Maintain a rotating bank of questions that can be posted independently of the
news cycle.

- Families: broad curiosity, constrained trade-off, hypothetical, nostalgia,
  local price comparison, career choice, card-community question, and light
  rage-bait.
- Guardrails: no fabricated premise, politics, tragedy, medical misinformation,
  or attack on a private person.
- Use sparingly enough that the account still has a clear expertise signal.

### 7. Reply radar for high-signal prompts

Find posts where a strong account asks for model feedback, explains a financial
mechanism, estimates a market, or presents a comparison MG can improve.

- Deliverable: target URL, why a reply is worthwhile, one concise suggested
  reply, supporting source if factual, and a note on the value MG adds.
- Priority examples: model-improvement questions, market-size threads, ETF or
  personal-finance explainers, and product claims with an India implication.

### 8. GitHub milestone and build-story lane

Use repository commits, pull requests, and workflow artifacts to identify real
milestones worth explaining publicly.

- Formats: what changed and why, a before-and-after workflow, a lesson from a
  builder frustration, a shipped capability demo, or a milestone with gratitude.
- Gate: a green workflow is not itself a product achievement. Explain verified
  user value and preserve the GitHub evidence.

## Needs real user experience

### 9. Diet for a day

Prepare a practical post using MG's actual meals, quantities, timing, and reason
for eating them.

- Ask for the real meal log before drafting.
- Calculate nutrition only from sourced product labels or a declared nutrition
  database, and label estimates.
- Do not turn it into medical advice.

### 10. Travel recap capture kit

Use only after an actual trip.

- Ask MG to capture total cost, major categories, booking method, cards or
  points actually used, photos, one surprise, and one mistake or catch.
- Possible outputs: concise cost recap, card/forex lesson, itinerary insight, or
  one destination-specific practical tip.
- Never infer that MG took a trip from a plan, booking search, or old photo.

### 11. Personal price-over-time observation

When MG notices a real price change, capture the old price, current price, city,
  approximate dates, and a photo if available. End with a natural local question.

### 12. Hands-on product opinion

Use a real photo or screen recording plus one direct opinion after MG has seen or
used the product. If he has not used it, frame the post as anticipation or ask a
question instead.

- Large threads containing dozens of videos.
- Generic before-and-after image comparisons with no distinct insight.
