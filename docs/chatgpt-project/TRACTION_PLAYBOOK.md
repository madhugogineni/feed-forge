# X traction playbook for the pilot

Updated 23 September 2026. Recheck platform behavior before treating any tactic
as permanent.

## What the strongest current evidence supports

X's published For You system retrieves recent posts from followed accounts and
out-of-network candidates, then predicts multiple viewer actions. Those actions
include likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares, post and profile clicks, media
opens, video quality views, dwell, author follows, and negative feedback. The
predictions are combined into a score. The published system also applies author
diversity, an out-of-network discount, a new-author boost, and visibility
filters. The weights apply to predicted behavior for the viewer, not raw global
counts.

Practical implication: there is no defensible recipe such as “one reply equals
N likes.” Create content that is relevant enough to earn several meaningful
actions from the right people. Avoid spam, near-duplicates, and content likely to
cause hides, mutes, blocks, or reports.

The published pipeline filters older inventory and already-seen posts and
includes a 48-hour age filter for For You candidates. Freshness therefore
matters, especially for news and launches, but a late post can still work when
it adds a useful catch or original explanation.

Official X guidance recommends a clear profile, content the audience cares
about, a natural conversational voice, replies to people who engage, and photos
or video when they help a post stand out. X's creative guidance favors a clear
connection between copy and media, mobile-readable visuals, and concise copy.

## Pilot practices

These are operating hypotheses to test, not platform guarantees:

1. Make replies the discovery engine. Reply early to relevant peers, growing
   creators, and high-reach accounts only when MG can add a fact, useful
   comparison, India-specific implication, honest question, correction, or real
   experience.
2. Give profile visitors a reason to follow. Maintain original posts that show a
   consistent cards, payments, technology, AI, and India perspective.
3. Use news plus the catch. State the change, then explain the cap, exclusion,
   fee, missing feature, or honest limit that changes the verdict.
4. Prefer specificity. Names, rupee values, deadlines, exact product behavior,
   and real screenshots beat vague commentary when they are verified.
5. Use one idea per post. Put the consequence or surprising point first.
6. Use two question lanes deliberately. A substantive question should help MG
   learn something or let an affected community compare experiences. An
   occasional broad, hypothetical, nostalgic, constrained-choice, or light
   rage-bait-style question may be used primarily for conversation, as MG
   explicitly approved on 23 September 2026. Never fabricate the premise or use
   politics, tragedy, medical misinformation, or attacks on a private person.
7. Reply to substantive responses. Conversation and relationships matter even
   when a reply is not broadly distributed in For You.
8. Avoid posting several similar items in a burst. Space distinct ideas and let
   the account analytics determine cadence.
9. Use media selectively:
   - text for a sharp take or question;
   - a real photo or screenshot for proof and hands-on moments;
   - a simple chart for one verified comparison;
   - an original illustration for an abstract mechanism;
   - a short video for a demo, timeline, or transformation that benefits from
     motion.
10. Measure what compounds: relevant follows, profile visits, substantive
    replies, saves, shares, and repeat interactions. Treat impressions and likes
    as supporting signals rather than the goal.

## Media defaults

- Image: 1:1 at 1080 x 1080 or 16:9 at 1920 x 1080, chosen for the content.
- Video: prefer 1:1 or 16:9 for the main feed and 9:16 only when the concept is
  designed for vertical viewing.
- Keep important text large and inside safe margins.
- Add objective alt text to every image. Include visible text and the important
  trend or relationship in a chart.
- Caption videos so the idea works without sound.
- Keep most pilot videos short. The goal is clarity and completion, not filling
  the maximum upload length.

Current X upload limits and exact specifications can change. Check the official
help and creative-spec pages before exporting a production asset:

## Experiments to run

Test one variable at a time over enough posts to avoid reacting to noise:

- text-only versus a useful image for the same content family;
- short direct take versus a structured explainer;
- news plus the catch versus plain news summary;
- real screenshot/photo versus generated illustration;
- posting windows derived from `@madhugogineni97` analytics in Asia/Kolkata;
- reply targets by account pool and relationship depth.

Record edits and results only when the user reports them or the system directly
observes them. Do not invent performance history.
