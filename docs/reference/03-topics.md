# Topic pillars

## Fast sources (sweep first, every run)

This is where news breaks before it trends on X. Check these at the start of every run, before the pillar searches. Flag anything under ~24 hours old that his niche on X hasn't covered well, and look for the catch that others missed. Pull the feeds listed under "Feeds to pull" below first, then check the pages that don't have feeds.

- **Telecom and app offers.** TelecomTalk, OnlyTech and Gadgets360, plus the newsrooms and offer pages of Jio, Airtel and Vi.
- **Cards and banking.**
  - Regulators: RBI press releases and notifications, NPCI press releases.
  - Card news sites: CardExpert, Card Insider, and the TechnoFino forum (leads only).
  - Issuer launch and MITC/T&C pages: HDFC, Axis, HSBC, ICICI, SBI Card, Amex and Scapia.
- **Apple and the device ecosystem.** (added 20 Sep 2026, for the @ManmohitSandhu lane — see pattern #14 in `05-great-posts.md`.) Apple stories that touch payments, cards or India pricing are MG's strongest crossover, so this gets its own sweep:
  - **Apple India Newsroom** (`apple.com/in/newsroom/rss-feed.rss`) — the primary source, and it's India-specific, so it catches things like iCloud+ bundling changes for India that the US feed doesn't lead with. **Verified working 20 Sep 2026.**
  - **9to5Mac** and **MacRumors** — fastest on Wallet, NFC, iOS beta strings and "which banks are live" details. 9to5Mac feed verified 20 Sep 2026.
  - **Apple's own support and availability pages** — the primary source that settles arguments: the Apple Pay participating banks page for India, and the iOS feature availability page. When a launch is rumoured, this is what confirms it actually shipped. No feed, check the page.
  - **@markgurman** (already on the watchlist) for what's coming, and his Sunday-evening Power On.
  - Rule for this lane: the rumour sites give the lead, Apple's own pages give the confirmation. Never post "it's live" off a rumour site alone.
- **India tech and payments policy.** (added 20 Sep 2026) **Medianama** (`medianama.com/feed/`, verified working) covers RBI and NPCI rules, UPI economics, TRAI and data policy in more depth than the general business press. This is the source that would have fed the UPI MDR post properly. Treat its policy reporting as a lead and confirm the rule itself on the RBI or NPCI page.
- **Finance: the volume source.** Moneycontrol publishes the most finance articles of any site here, so go through its latest news plus these sections:
  - personal finance, banking, markets, economy, tech and startups
  - its travel and auto sections, which are useful for the travel and deals pillars

  Also check ET (Wealth, Markets, Banking/Finance, Tech), LiveMint (Money, Markets, Companies) and Business Standard (Finance, Markets).
- **Travel and visas.**
  - India aviation and loyalty: Live From A Lounge (India-focused, covering airline, loyalty and lounge changes), ET TravelWorld, and the Mint and Moneycontrol travel sections.
  - Global airline and loyalty changes: One Mile at a Time and Simple Flying.
  - Official visa sources, which are the ones to verify against:
    - US: travel.state.gov (visa news, visa bulletin, appointment wait times)
    - UK: gov.uk visas and immigration news
    - Schengen: the individual embassy sites; Schengen Visa Info is fine for news leads
    - Canada IRCC newsroom, Australia Home Affairs, and the UAE ICP and GDRFA pages
    - VFS Global news and advisories for India
  - Indian passport access: Passport Index and the Henley Passport Index for rankings, and the "Visa requirements for Indian citizens" Wikipedia page as a quick reference for recent changes.
  - Indian side: MEA press releases (non-political items only), the Bureau of Immigration for e-visa changes for visitors to India, DGCA, and the Air India, IndiGo and Akasa newsrooms.
  - **Leads only:** aggregator travel sites (Travel And Tour World and similar) repost fast but get details wrong. Always confirm a visa change on the embassy or immigration site before posting.
- **Deals.** DesiDime, TechnoFino Deals and Telegram deal channels (leads only). Use Keepa or PriceHistory for price checks.
- **Gadgets.** 91mobiles, Smartprix and Beebom, plus @stufflistings and @yabhishekhd on X.
- **AI.** The OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta blogs, TestingCatalog, and @kimmonismus on X.
- **Startups and business.** Inc42, Entrackr, Moneycontrol, ET Tech, and PIB (non-political items only).
- **Fast repackagers on X, used as a benchmark.** @mufaddal_vohra, @Ravisutanjani and @ccg33k. If one of them has posted something MG hasn't covered, that's a gap to fill.

**Being late is fine if the post adds something.** On 20 Sep 2026, Ravisutanjani's Jio–Canva post got 17K views even though it went up 4 days after TelecomTalk broke the story. What it skipped was the catch: you must keep an active Jio 5G plan for all 12 months. Timing and packaging for the audience matter more than being first, and adding the catch or his take is what makes MG's version worth posting.

## Anchor pillars (every day)

**1. Credit cards in India.** Launches, upcoming cards, devaluations, milestone and lounge changes, fee waivers, forex markup, RBI card rules, and #ccgeeks questions. Always check for **new or upcoming cards** and flag whether they're any good.
- His angles: Axis Atlas, HDFC Diners Black Metal, HSBC Premier, and his evaluation of Scapia.
- Sources: issuer T&C / MITC PDFs (primary), RBI, NPCI, CardExpert, TechnoFino (leads only), LiveMint, Moneycontrol.

**2. Deals and vouchers.**

*Tech and gadget deals (the lead item).* Phones, laptops, earbuds, watches, TVs, accessories and smart-home gear that are genuinely at a great price. Look on **any site**: Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales, brand stores (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi), Tata Neu, JioMart, Myntra, Ajio, Blinkit/Zepto electronics, refurbished/pre-owned stores, and deal communities (DesiDime, TechnoFino, Telegram deal channels as leads).
- A deal is "great" when the effective price after bank offers is at or near the lowest you can find. Check price history (e.g. Keepa or PriceHistory) where possible and say "lowest in X months" only if the tracker shows it.
- Always show the maths: listed price → bank or card offer → effective price.

*Vouchers and bank offers, for all banks.* Cover every Indian issuer, not just the cards MG holds:
- HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, IDFC FIRST, IndusInd, AU, Yes, RBL, HSBC, Standard Chartered, Federal, BOB, Amex, OneCard, Scapia and others.
- Also cover their reward portals and voucher stores: HDFC SmartBuy, Axis Grab Deals / EDGE Rewards, ICICI iShop, HSBC Unicorn / exclusive rewards, Amex Reward Multiplier, SBI Rewardz, Kotak and IDFC portals.
- Include gift-voucher platforms (Gyftr, Amazon Pay vouchers, Swiggy/Zomato vouchers, Flipkart vouchers) and which card earns the most on each.
- Useful formats: "which card to use for this sale", "this voucher + this card = X% back", and "this offer has a catch".

*Rules.* Verify every price, offer and end date on the live page. Say when the deal expires. No affiliate links until ~2,000 followers.

**3. AI.** Model and tool launches, coding agents, AI in India, and AI's real effect on jobs and IT.

**4. Tech and gadgets.** Phones, laptops, chips, wearables and cool gadgets, focusing on India prices and launches.

**5. Payment rails and the device ecosystem.** (added 20 Sep 2026 — MG asked for "that side of the content" after @ManmohitSandhu's Apple Pay post.) This is the crossover between pillars 1 and 4, and it's the one MG is best placed to own, because most Apple accounts don't know cards and most cards accounts don't know the hardware.
- What belongs here: Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay in India; tokenisation and what actually leaves the phone; NFC and tap-to-pay rollouts; UPI on wearables and UPI Lite; RBI rules on biometric or card-on-file payments; NPCI's UPI tap and its economics (MDR); which issuers go live first and why; eSIM and Wallet features that land in India late or not at all.
- **How to write it:** pattern #14 in `05-great-posts.md`. Correct the wrong frame first, spec out what lands *and what doesn't*, then the regulator groundwork, then his own stake, then an honest limit.
- **The rule that keeps it credible:** separate "reported" from "shipped". Rumour sites and issuer leaks give the lead; the Apple/Google availability page, the issuer's own announcement, or an RBI/NPCI circular confirms it. Say which one you're standing on.
- **His stake is real:** he holds an Axis Atlas, so on the Axis-first Apple Pay rollout he is genuinely in the first wave. Use first-person where it's true, never where it isn't.
- Sources: Apple India Newsroom, Apple Pay participating banks page, 9to5Mac/MacRumors, NPCI, RBI, Medianama, issuer newsrooms, and @iSatishAgarwal / @ccg33k / @suritalreja on X for the cards-side reaction.

## Rotating pillars (a few times a week each)

- **Travel, visas and lifestyle.** Mostly international trips, some within India. Cover forex, lounges, points redemptions, hotel and airline changes, and **visa changes that affect Indian passport holders**: new e-visas or ETAs, visa-free entry added or removed, shorter permitted stays, fee hikes, appointment wait times, and transit visa rules. Pairs well with cards (forex markup, lounge access, travel insurance). Sources are under "Travel and visas" above. Always verify on the official site.
- **Diet and workouts.** Gym and weekend walks. Use lived-experience observations and honest study reads ("linked to" does not mean "causes").
- **Investing.** Indian markets, US and international investing from India, LRS/TCS, mutual fund flows, the rupee. No buy/sell calls.
- **Money lessons and personal finance basics.** (added 20 Sep 2026 — MG's pick, @suritalreja.) Plain-English things people get wrong with money: EMIs that feel affordable, lifestyle creep, how credit cards actually work, why the minimum due is a trap, taxes people learn late. No research needed and no sources to verify, which is the point: this is the low-effort, human-sounding lane that sits between the heavy posts. Write it as pattern #15. Keep it to about one a week.
- **Indian marketing and local business.** D2C brand plays, clever or bad campaigns, quick commerce, and local business moves.
- **India (non-political).** Infrastructure, startups, UPI, telecom, tax changes.
- **Hyderabad: weather, development and city life.** Local posts earn loyal local followers, and the practical ones get shared. Check this pillar every run and post from it a few times a week, more often when something's happening.
  - **Weather.** Rain alerts, heat waves, flooding hotspots and waterlogged roads, AQI, and seasonal shifts. Keep it practical, e.g. "leave early, Hitech City underpasses flood after 30 mins of rain". Sources: IMD Hyderabad, the Telangana State Development Planning Society (TSDPS) weather updates, GHMC alerts, and local weather accounts on X (e.g. @Hyderabadrains).
  - **Ongoing development.** Metro Phase 2 and airport metro, new flyovers and underpasses, road widening, ORR/RRR, Musi riverfront, the Future City / Fourth City plans, new IT parks and GCC offices, big real estate projects, and airport expansion. Cover the practical impact (commute, closures, timelines), not the politics of who announced it. Sources: HMRL, HMDA, GHMC, TOI Hyderabad, The Hindu Hyderabad, Deccan Chronicle, Telangana Today, Siasat (non-political items only).
  - **Events.** Concerts, stand-up, food festivals, marathons, cricket at Uppal, tech meetups and conferences, festivals (Bonalu, Bathukamma, Ganesh), exhibitions, and weekend markets. Sources: District by Zomato, BookMyShow, Instagram/X event pages, and venue accounts.
  - **New cafés and restaurants.** New openings, notable menus, brunch spots, and places worth the hype or not. Areas: Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Kokapet, Financial District. Sources: Zomato/Swiggy new listings, local food creators on X and Instagram, and Hyderabad food blogs. Never claim he visited somewhere unless he says so. Ask instead ("have you tried X?"), or post it as a question ("anyone been to X yet? worth it?").
  - **Everyday city life.** Traffic, commutes, rents, the IT corridor, and Hyderabad vs Bangalore takes.
  - **Fits well with other pillars.** Card offers at Hyderabad restaurants (dining programmes, EazyDiner, Swiggy Dineout), and deals at local stores.
- **Software jobs and Indian IT.** Hiring, layoffs, results, appraisals, H-1B.
- **Health and ergonomics.** Desk-work health, Frido and ergonomic D2C.
- **Marvel and TV shows.** Releases, India premiere dates, box office. Mostly for evenings and weekends.
