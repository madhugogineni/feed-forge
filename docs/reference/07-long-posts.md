# Long-post style guide

**Every long post (over 280 characters, needs Premium) uses this shape** unless MG asks for something else. Set 21 Sep 2026, when MG pointed at @AmazingCreditC's Visa offers post ([link](https://x.com/AmazingCreditC/status/2101941947808309464)) and said this is "the type of posts I would love to post."

A long post is a scannable guide that people save and forward. It isn't an essay or a thread of stats. Short takes, jokes, questions and reactions stay short and keep following `05-great-posts.md`.

## The shape, top to bottom

1. **Headline line.** An emoji pair (🚨💳, ✈️💳, 📱💳) and one plain sentence saying what this is and why now.
   - Model: "🚨💳 Two excellent offers are currently running for Visa cardholders in India, and both are expiring soon."
2. **Who it's for,** written to the reader in one or two lines.
   - Model: "If you have travelled internationally, are paying education expenses abroad, or received a new Visa credit card recently, these are worth checking."
3. **Numbered sections, each with an emoji header on its own line:** "✈️ 1. Visa Power Travel", "💳 2. Visa New Card Issuance Offer". Sub-sections get an emoji header too ("🎯 Jetsetter Milestone Bonus").
4. **The deadline on its own line** under the header, e.g. "Valid till 30 September 2026."
5. **One fact per short paragraph,** with a blank line between every paragraph. No paragraph runs past 2–3 lines.
6. **"Here's how you can benefit:" followed by • bullets.** Each bullet is a full, plain sentence. Lists of tiers or prices can be written as "• Visa Signature: ₹500 voucher". This is the only place value lists with colons are allowed, because they're a real table of values and not a bot-style answer.
7. **🎯 The catch, in its own section.** Caps, deposit or spend conditions, exclusions, and when rewards expire. MG's #1 pattern still applies inside the long format.
8. **🧮 Does it pay for itself?** Add this when there's a fee. Keep it to two lines of maths, run in code, with every input taken from the issuer's page.
9. **💡 Tip or 💡 Pro tip.** A practical way to get more out of the offer, with a worked example ("So for example, if you…"). This section is what earns saves.
10. **🙋 My take.** Two or three lines in first person. The model post has almost none of this, but MG's voice rule still applies, so every post carries his take. Use only facts about him that are on file, and ask him when you need one.
11. **Where to check or claim it,** as a plain link. No affiliate links until ~2,000 followers.
12. **🗓️ Quick recap.** The 2–4 numbers or dates that matter, each on its own line starting with an emoji.
13. **An optional one-line call to action,** e.g. "If you are eligible, make sure you claim before they end."

## Tone and rules

- Calm and helpful, like a friend who read the T&C for you. No hype words and no em dashes.
- Emojis mark the sections, plus 1–2 more in the body.
- Every number comes from the issuer's own page or T&C. When the live page and a T&C PDF disagree, go with the live page and note the mismatch in the doc.
- Aim for about 1,500–3,000 characters. Tag the draft `[needs Premium]` and also give an under-280 teaser that can be posted without Premium or used as a quote-tweet.
- **Best for:** card and debit card launches, bank offers with deadlines, sale bank-offer guides ("which card for BBD"), devaluations with several changes, and forex or travel offers.
- **Not for:** takes, jokes, questions or quick reactions.
- How the model post did: about 1.5K views within the first hour on a 37.6K account. Posts like this get their value from bookmarks and forwards more than from likes.

## Worked example: Kotak811 Infinity Metal debit card (21 Sep 2026)

Sources: the [Kotak811 product page](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/debit-cards/infinity-metal-debit-card) (fees by colour, benefits), the [811 Super page](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/savings-account/811-super-savings-account) (5% up to ₹500 a month, ₹10,000 single deposit rule, claim by month end), and the [April 2026 T&C PDF](https://images.kotak.bank.in/bank/mailers/2026/files/811_MetalDC_TnC_Apr_26.pdf) (₹1,999 anniversary fee, insurance activation rule). The PDF lists older issuance fees (₹2,999 / ₹4,999), so the post uses the live page (₹3,999 / ₹4,999).

```
🚨💳 Kotak811 now has a metal debit card, and it comes in 5 colours.

If you bank with Kotak811, or you've wanted a metal card without taking on another credit card, here's what it gives you and what it really costs.

💰 1. What it costs

The fee depends on the colour you pick.

• Gold: ₹4,999
• Silver, Rose Gold, Midnight Black and Crimson Red: ₹3,999

It drops to ₹1,999 for Gold and ₹1,499 for the others if you make a one-time deposit of ₹1,81,100 (Gold) or ₹81,100 (others).

From the second year, the renewal fee is ₹1,999.

You'll need an 811 Super account to get it.

✨ 2. What you get

• 5% cashback on card spends, up to ₹500 a month. That's up to ₹6,000 a year.

• 4 domestic airport lounge visits a year, one every quarter.

• Buy 1 get 1 on BookMyShow, up to ₹300 off, once a month.

• 20% off on Zomato Dining, up to ₹750, once a month on a bill of ₹2,000 or more.

• 2% forex markup on international spends, vs up to 3.5% on many other cards.

• Fuel surcharge waiver, plus ₹1 crore air accident cover, lost card and lost baggage cover.

🎯 The catch

The 5% cashback only applies in months where you deposit ₹10,000 into the account in a single transaction. Miss that and you lose that month's cashback.

Only card swipes and online payments count. ATM withdrawals don't. And you have to claim the cashback in the 811 app by the end of the next month, or it expires.

The insurance also needs at least 2 card spends of ₹500 each in the last 30 days to stay active.

🧮 Does it pay for itself?

To hit the ₹500 cap, you need to spend ₹10,000 a month on the card.

Do that every month and you get ₹6,000 back against a ₹1,999 renewal fee. That's ₹4,001 in your pocket from year two.

💡 Tip: Set up a monthly auto-transfer of ₹10,000 from your salary account to your 811 account as one single transfer. That way you never miss the deposit rule, and every month's cashback stays live.

🙋 My take

The lounge visits don't do much for me, since my Diners Black already covers lounges. But if you don't want a credit card, or you're just starting out, this is one of the better debit cards you can carry right now.

🗓️ Quick recap

💳 Fee: ₹3,999 / ₹4,999 (₹1,499 / ₹1,999 with the deposit)
🔁 Renewal: ₹1,999
💰 Cashback: up to ₹500 a month, only with a ₹10,000 deposit that month
✈️ Lounges: 1 per quarter, domestic only

Check it out here: kotak811.bank.in/debit-cards/infinity-metal-debit-card
```
