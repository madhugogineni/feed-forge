2. **Useful small tool or skill.** An evidence checker, offer-catch extractor,
   benchmark comparison helper, post-package helper, or chart tool. Prefer

3. **Model-release comparison.** Compare capabilities, price, limits, availability,
   benchmark provenance, and one equivalent alternative; distinguish provider

5. **Consumer-tech market-share watch.** Canalys and other original market reports,
   with period, geography, and measurement definition preserved.
6. **Question bank.** Curiosity, career/money trade-offs, hypotheticals, nostalgia,
   local prices, and cards/technology preferences that are easy to answer.
7. **High-signal reply radar.** Model feedback, financial mechanisms, market-size
   claims, and product comparisons where MG can contribute something specific.
8. **GitHub build story.** A real capability, lesson, or milestone supported by
   commits, PRs, and outputs. Explain user value rather than celebrating a green job.
